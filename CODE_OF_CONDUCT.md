# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a welcoming, respectful,
and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Assume good intent and discuss technical disagreements professionally.
- Respect project boundaries, including the synthetic-only data policy.
- Protect confidential, personal, and proprietary information.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, sexualized conduct,
unwelcome attention, doxxing, and sharing confidential or proprietary information
are not acceptable. Deliberately bypassing project safety or data-boundary rules is
also unacceptable.

## Scope and Enforcement

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces and whenever someone is representing
the project. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or take other
appropriate action in response to unacceptable behavior.

Please report concerns privately to the project maintainers through GitHub rather
than posting sensitive details in a public issue. Reports will be reviewed promptly
and handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant][covenant], version 2.1.
